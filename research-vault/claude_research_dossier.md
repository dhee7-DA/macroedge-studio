## 🏛️ 1. Institutional Profile
*   **Company**: Anthropic PBC (Public Benefit Corporation)
*   **Founded**: 2021 by Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei (Ex-OpenAI VP of Research/Safety).
*   **Core Philosophy**: **Constitutional AI**. A "Safety-First" approach where models are trained against a set of explicit principles (The "Constitution") rather than purely through human feedback (RLHF).
*   **Strategic Partners**: Amazon (Primary Cloud Partner), Google (Strategic Equity Partner).

*   **Claude Cowork**: Integrated desktop agent with "Computer Use" capabilities (Screenshots, Clicking, Typing).

### **C. The "Black Box" (Project Glasswing)**
*   **Claude Mythos (Capybara)**: An unreleased frontier model that outperformed all competitors in autonomous vulnerability detection.
*   **Decision**: Anthropic has **refused public release** due to cyber-misuse risks.
*   **Glasswing Initiative**: A defensive coalition (AWS, Apple, Google, Microsoft) using Mythos Preview to patch zero-day flaws before hackers can exploit them.

## 🛡️ 5. The "Constitutional" Difference
