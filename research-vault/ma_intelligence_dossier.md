## ⚡ 2. The "Shadow Acquisition" Model (Energy)
Major tech firms are moving away from buying energy startups outright and instead using **Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs)** to achieve 100% control without balance-sheet risk.

*   **Microsoft & Helion**: No acquisition, but Microsoft has secured a PPA for 100% of the first 50MW output. This is a "De-Facto" control of the asset.
*   **Google & CFS**: Google signed a 200MW PPA with **Commonwealth Fusion Systems (CFS)** in April 2026. 
*   **Institutional Insight**: Big Tech is effectively funding the CAPEX of these firms in exchange for exclusive, fixed-price energy moats.
