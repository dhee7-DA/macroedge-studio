### 1. Executive Summary
On April 28, 2026, the United Arab Emirates announced its formal withdrawal from OPEC and the broader OPEC+ alliance, effective May 1, 2026 — ending a **58-year membership**. This is the most significant structural rupture in the cartel's history. The UAE, OPEC's third-largest producer, has chosen sovereign production autonomy over collective supply discipline at a time of acute regional geopolitical instability and an ongoing Strait of Hormuz disruption.

This report analyzes the **cartel mechanics**, **ADNOC production roadmap**, **global oil price scenarios**, and the **strategic winners and losers** of this historic pivot.
