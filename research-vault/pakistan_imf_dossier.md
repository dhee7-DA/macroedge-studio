### 5. The Doom Loop — 7 Steps

1. **Forex Crisis**: Import bills exceed reserves. Currency collapses.
2. **IMF Call**: Emergency program negotiated. Conditions attached.
3. **Austerity**: Subsidies cut, energy prices raised, taxes hiked.
4. **Consumer Pain**: Inflation spikes. Unemployment rises. Protests begin.
5. **Political Backlash**: Opposition promises relief. Government reverses reforms to survive.
6. **Structural Decay**: Underlying problems worsen. Tax base doesn't grow. Circular debt expands.
7. **Next Crisis**: In 2–3 years, reserves drop again. Step 1 repeats.
